How Notifications Work

  • Only finalized yearly plans trigger notifications.
  • Notifications are sent once per plan (duplicate prevention for 24 hours).
  • To enable automatic checks, set up a cron job to call /notifications/check daily.
  • Example cron: 0 8 * * * curl -s http://localhost:5000/notifications/check
